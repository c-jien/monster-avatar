Monster avatars

Placeholder profile pictures generated from a name. Each name always gets the same monster, and no two names are meant to look alike. Nothing is stored or random: the name is hashed, the hash seeds each trait, and the traits are drawn as a 100×100 SVG that the existing round avatar slot crops.

How it works

" Ian CHENG " ─normalize─▶ "ian cheng" ─FNV-1a+fmix(key·trait)─▶ one mulberry32 stream per trait ─▶ traits + continuous params ─▶ <svg>

Deterministic

Integer-only hashing and a seeded PRNG. No Math.random, no DOM, so the server, the browser and tests all produce the same bytes. Case and whitespace are normalized first.

Stable per trait

Each trait (eyes, mouth, hat…) has its own seed, hash(name + trait). Adding a new hat later only changes some hats. It doesn't reshuffle everyone's eyes or colours.

Many variations

Body kind × blob shape × 5 eye layouts × 8 eye styles × 10 mouths × 11 top features × 11 hats × face, neck and ear accessories × 7 patterns × 5 backdrops, plus continuous hue, proportions, outline wobble, gaze and tilt.

Built for a circle

Portrait framing: head or head-and-shoulders, with key features inside the inscribed circle. Large eyes, a consistent ink outline and flat shading keep it readable at 24px.

Harmonious colour

Hue comes from the hash. Saturation and lightness are clamped (no olive mud) and the backdrop uses an analogous, complementary or split hue. An optional dark-background variant suits dark mode.

Drop-in

Returns an SVG string, about 3–8 KB. In the app, render it as <img src={monsterAvatarDataUri(name)}> wherever the letter fallback is now. The markup doesn't change.
