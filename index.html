How it works

" Ian CHENG " ─normalize─▶ "ian cheng" ─FNV-1a+fmix(key·trait)─▶ one mulberry32 stream per trait ─▶ traits + continuous params ─▶ <svg>

Deterministic

Integer-only hashing and a seeded PRNG. No Math.random, no DOM, so rendering is repeatable without a server or image downloads. Case and whitespace are normalized first.

Stable per trait

Each trait (eyes, mouth, hat…) has its own seed, hash(name + trait). Overriding a mouth keeps unrelated generated traits stable. Related details adjust when needed to fit together.

Many variations

Mix bodies, eyes, mouths, hats, accessories and patterns with procedural proportions and color. Avatars share a style, with plenty of variety. Different seeds are not guaranteed to look unique.

Built for a circle

Portrait framing: head or head-and-shoulders, with key features inside the inscribed circle. Large eyes, a consistent ink outline and flat shading keep it readable at 24px.

Harmonious colour

Hue comes from the hash. Saturation and lightness are clamped (no olive mud) and the backdrop uses an analogous, complementary or split hue. An optional dark-background variant suits dark mode.

Drop-in

Returns an SVG string and a saved configuration. In the app, render it as <img src={monsterAvatarDataUri(name)}> wherever the letter fallback is now. The markup doesn't change.
